# Pretrained audio engine research — 23 November 2026

No pretrained transformer was evaluated or deployed in this run. The local environment has no PyTorch, Transformers or FunASR. Avoid adding these as mandatory app dependencies without an independent evaluation and packaging trial. CPU latency and peak RAM/VRAM for the models below remain **unmeasured** here; parameter/weight sizes do not establish latency. GPU fine-tuning on the 6 GB laptop was not attempted.

| Candidate and primary source | Architecture/data/labels | License and download | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| [SUPERB wav2vec2-base ER](https://huggingface.co/superb/wav2vec2-base-superb-er) | wav2vec2-base, 16 kHz; emotion head trained for four IEMOCAP classes (anger, happiness, sadness, neutral) | Apache 2.0; official repository weight file about 378 MB | Plausible CPU/GPU research engine, but cannot replace the eight-class app by inventing Fearful/Disgust/Surprised/Calm outputs. A future four-common-class comparison must restrict both models to the same examples/labels and report coverage. Not downloaded or benchmarked. |
| [emotion2vec+ seed](https://huggingface.co/emotion2vec/emotion2vec_plus_seed) | Self-supervised speech representation plus emotion head; 201 hours academic EmoBox data; angry/disgusted/fearful/happy/neutral/other/sad/surprised/unknown | Custom model-license metadata; official file about 1.12 GB; FunASR/ModelScope runtime | Seven app labels map directly, Calm has no counterpart, Other/Unknown must remain explicit. Academic training-corpus overlap must be audited before claiming RAVDESS/EmoDB independence. Deferred rather than reporting contaminated accuracy. The model-card inference specification mentions 4096 MB memory, but this is not a measured laptop RAM requirement. |
| [audEERING wav2vec2 MSP dimensional](https://huggingface.co/audeering/wav2vec2-large-robust-12-ft-emotion-msp-dim) | Wav2vec2-Large-Robust pruned to 12 layers; MSP-Podcast v1.7; arousal/dominance/valence outputs and embeddings | CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 research release; ONNX export linked in card | Continuous dimensions cannot be directly relabeled as the eight categorical emotions. Would need a separately trained and validated categorical head. Not downloaded. |

WavLM and HuBERT representation models are also possible embedding sources, but general speech pretraining alone is not an emotion classifier. Installing more backbones without a controlled labeled evaluation would not establish an improvement. The next justified research step is an optional isolated PyTorch environment, a small fixed four-class SUPERB trial, and separate unseen speakers from an external corpus. All future fine-tuning must group original recordings and their augmentations together. Full-precision weight sizes imply hundreds of MB to over 1 GB of model storage alone; activations, decoder and runtime add overhead, especially in training. No numeric latency or VRAM promise is made.

Dataset opportunities were rechecked: [CREMA-D](https://github.com/CheyneyComputerScience/CREMA-D) offers audiovisual data and actor IDs, with ODbL/DbCL notices and an author-requested usage form; it omits Calm and Surprise. [SAVEE](https://cvssp.org/savee/Register.html) requires registration/license acceptance and has only four male speakers. [IEMOCAP](https://sail.usc.edu/iemocap/Data_Release_Form_IEMOCAP.pdf) requires an academic release agreement; excitement and frustration need an explicit mapping policy. None was silently merged. TESS's two speakers and EmoDB's acted German speech remain narrow transfer checks. Byte hashes detect exact duplicates, not all acoustically near-identical recordings. A robust near-duplicate audit remains future work.
